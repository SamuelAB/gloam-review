Gloam VR: rain studies

Gloam VR is a walkable virtual-reality sculpture by Samuel Arsenault-Brassard: a pierced stone dome you explore on foot, crouching and sitting inside it. These pages follow work in progress on bringing weather into it, starting with rain that behaves like real water on real stone.

The rain is our own simulation, written for the graphics card: every drop falls at the speed its size dictates, lands, beads, creeps, slides, merges and drips; the stone remembers where water has run, drinks it into its pores and dries over time; and the floor collects what reaches it. Detail is spent where a visitor inside can actually see it.
